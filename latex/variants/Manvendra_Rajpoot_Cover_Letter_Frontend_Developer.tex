Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the \RoleTitle{} role on your team. I build interfaces for
products where the frontend carries real weight: billing consoles, cloud control panels,
offline-capable apps and fast, accessible marketing sites. Because I also build the
APIs behind them, I design screens around how the data actually behaves, not around a
mock.

At CloudTechTiq I built the customer and admin console for a cloud hosting and billing
platform in React 18, Redux Toolkit and Vite, across more than 35 feature modules: billing
and invoices, orders and carts, CloudStack resources, tickets, staff roles, tax and payment
settings, and monitoring dashboards in ApexCharts. I shipped an in-browser virtual-machine
console on xterm.js, Razorpay checkout, Microsoft sign-in through MSAL, Cloudflare
Turnstile, admin impersonation and sanitised rich-text content, all behind axios
interceptors that handle retries and silent JWT refresh. I also built the company's
marketing site in Next.js and TypeScript with Tailwind and Radix/shadcn components, and
the Workforce HR web app in React 19, TypeScript and Tailwind v4 with i18n, a documented
design system, and Vitest and Playwright tests.

I have built offline-first patterns in React 19 and TypeScript: TanStack Query
over an IndexedDB outbox that replays writes when the connection returns, installable as
a progressive web app, and covered by Vitest unit tests and Playwright end-to-end suites
against the real API. I keep frontend and backend in lockstep with typed TypeScript API
layers, schema-validated contracts in Zod and Pydantic, and OpenAPI specs, so a contract
change fails in CI rather than in a customer's browser. For public pages I ship fast
static surfaces in Next.js and Astro with generated Open Graph images, RSS and sitemaps.

My portfolio, \href{https://www.rajpoot.dev}{rajpoot.dev}, shows how I treat
quality on the web. It is Next.js 16, React 19 and strict TypeScript, statically
prerendered, with a strict Content Security Policy, JSON-LD and Lighthouse CI budgets on
script and transfer size. CI runs ESLint, Vitest with a coverage gate, Playwright across
Chromium, WebKit and mobile, CodeQL and visual regression. I care about accessibility,
reduced motion, and keeping the JavaScript a page ships as small as it can be.

Because I own the backend too (Django REST Framework, FastAPI, payments and
authentication), I can carry a feature from schema to pixel and spot contract problems
before they reach the user. I am comfortable reading a
Django serializer or a FastAPI schema, proposing the change the interface needs, and
shipping both sides together, which keeps the frontend honest about loading, error and
empty states instead of designing only for the happy path.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how this experience fits your team, and I am
happy to walk through any of the systems above in technical detail. Thank you for your
time and consideration.
