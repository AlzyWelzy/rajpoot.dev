Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the \RoleTitle{} role on your team. I come to infrastructure
as an engineer who also writes the services running on it, so I care about how a platform
is built, shipped, observed and recovered as much as how it is coded. I work daily with
Docker and containerised deployments, CI/CD, Linux and Kubernetes concepts, and I would
like to make platform work my main focus.

At CloudTechTiq I own the Docker Compose topology for our cloud console: 35 services
covering the Django portal, the CloudStack and billing FastAPI microservices, Celery
workers and Beat, PostgreSQL, Redis, RabbitMQ, MinIO, NGINX and the observability stack.
I wrapped it in one idempotent \texttt{make dev} that checks out submodules, rebuilds
images only when a Dockerfile or lockfile changed, waits until every application answers,
applies pending migrations and prints a health report, so a warm start takes about four
seconds. I built GitHub Actions pipelines that run pytest, build multi-stage images and
publish them to GHCR for dev, staging and production, with a separate migrations workflow
and container health checks behind Gunicorn and NGINX.

I built the observability platform from the ground up: OpenTelemetry instrumentation
of FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Redis, Celery and httpx, routed through an OTel Collector and
Grafana Alloy into Prometheus, Loki and Tempo, so a failure anywhere traces back to a
single request. I made environments reproducible with database seed snapshots and
code-defined reference data, and automated bare-metal provisioning over HPE iLO Redfish
with Celery-driven IP allocation. Behind it I designed the services themselves, including
the Razorpay and Stripe payment flow and an OAuth2 gateway to Apache CloudStack.

I have also run edge and cloud infrastructure end to end: Cloudflare DNS, Pages and
Workers, mail authentication with SPF, DKIM and DMARC at \texttt{p=reject}, uptime
monitoring, and written decision records and runbooks. I have taken deployments from a
GCP VM running a single-node Docker Swarm behind a cloudflared tunnel with automatic
image roll-outs, through managed platforms, to serverless, weighing cost, limits and
operability at each step. Every migration had a written cutover plan with a check and a
rollback per step, and background work runs with leases, heartbeats, retries and
dead-letter queues so failures surface instead of disappearing.

I treat infrastructure as something to be explained as well as run: every change has a
check, a rollback and a written reason, which is what lets a small team operate it safely. I am
keen to deepen my work with Kubernetes and infrastructure as code in a team that runs
platforms at a larger scale.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how this experience fits your team, and I am
happy to walk through any of the systems above in technical detail. Thank you for your
time and consideration.
