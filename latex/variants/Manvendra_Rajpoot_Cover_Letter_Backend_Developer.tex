Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the \RoleTitle{} role on your team. I build and own backend
systems where correctness is not optional: platforms that move money, provision
infrastructure, enforce access, and stay observable under load. I have taken these
systems from architecture to production, both at CloudTechTiq and on my own product,
and that is the work I want to keep doing at a larger scale.

At CloudTechTiq I architected a multi-tenant hosting and billing/ERP platform on
Django REST Framework spanning more than 25 apps and 230 ViewSets under one unified API.
The part I am most confident about is the money path. I designed a dual-gateway payment
system across Razorpay and Stripe with HMAC-SHA256 webhook idempotency to stop duplicate
charges, wired through invoicing, provisioning and double-entry ledger reconciliation,
plus Indian GST e-invoicing against the NIC gateway with IRN generation and signed QR
codes. As the platform grew I extracted billing into its own FastAPI microservice, the
single billing authority for every product: a double-entry ledger, payments allocated
across multiple invoices with refunds, proforma-to-final invoice posting, and
multi-entity issuance that resolves the legal entity, currency and GST/VAT from the
customer's billing address.

I also built the service that sits between customers and the cloud: an OAuth2/OpenID
authorization server and FastAPI gateway to Apache CloudStack for virtual machines,
volumes, networks and templates. It syncs state webhook-first with a polling fallback
and streams job progress to the browser over Server-Sent Events. Around it I shipped RS512
JWT authentication with kid-keyed JWKS and refresh-token rotation, two-factor sign-in over
email OTP and TOTP, a 43-permission department-scoped RBAC, real-time notifications over
Django Channels and Redis, and a Celery system with late acknowledgement and worker-lost
requeue. I removed N+1 queries across an async and sync database layer, and set up
OpenTelemetry tracing into Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and Tempo so any failure traces back
to a single request.

I have also learned to treat change itself as engineering work. I plan migrations
and cutovers with written runbooks that carry a check and a rollback for every step,
record architecture decisions so they are argued on evidence rather than memory, and gate
releases on a test-parity check so every existing test is ported or deliberately
dropped. I have hardened sign-in with passkeys (WebAuthn), TOTP, recovery codes and device
trust, and designed scheduled and queued work with leases, heartbeats, retries and
dead-letter queues so a failed job surfaces instead of disappearing. I also publish
open-source Python work, including a typed Namecheap SDK on PyPI.

Across all of this the throughline is ownership: I take complex,
correctness-critical backend systems and make them reliable, observable and
maintainable, and I document the decisions so the next engineer does not have to guess.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how this experience fits your team, and I am
happy to walk through any of the systems above in technical detail. Thank you for your
time and consideration.
