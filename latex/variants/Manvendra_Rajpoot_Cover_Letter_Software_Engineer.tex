Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the \RoleTitle{} role on your team. I work across the whole
system: Python and TypeScript services, React interfaces, containerised infrastructure
and the observability that ties them together. I am at my best owning a problem from
design to production rather than one layer of it.

At CloudTechTiq I architected a multi-tenant hosting and billing/ERP platform on Django
REST Framework, more than 25 apps and 230 ViewSets, and designed its Razorpay and Stripe
payment system with HMAC-SHA256 webhook idempotency, double-entry ledger reconciliation and
GST e-invoicing. I extracted billing into a dedicated FastAPI microservice and built an
OAuth2/OpenID gateway to Apache CloudStack that streams job progress over Server-Sent
Events. I secured the platform with RS512 JWT and JWKS, two-factor sign-in and a
43-permission RBAC, and built real-time notifications over Django Channels and Celery.

Beyond the services, I built the React and Redux Toolkit console customers and staff
use every day, and I own the Docker Compose topology of 35 services that runs the whole
platform locally, with GitHub Actions pipelines publishing images to GHCR. I set up
OpenTelemetry tracing into Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and Tempo so any failure traces back
to a single request, and removed N+1 queries across the data layer.

I have also built typed TypeScript API layers with Zod and Pydantic contracts and
OpenAPI specs, offline-first React 19 patterns with an IndexedDB outbox, and run edge and
cloud infrastructure on Cloudflare and GCP, moving deployments from a single-node Docker
Swarm VM to serverless as the trade-offs between cost, limits and operability changed.
Each migration had a written cutover plan with a check and a rollback for every step,
and releases were gated on a test-parity check proving every existing test was ported
or deliberately dropped.

Whichever layer the problem lives in, I bring the same habits: design the contract
first, make the risky paths correct and observable, and write the decisions down. I
read code more than I write it, I review carefully, and I am comfortable being the person
who is paged for a system and knows why it was built the way it was. I am looking for a
team where that kind of end-to-end ownership is valued, and where I can keep growing
across backend, frontend and platform work.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how this experience fits your team, and I am
happy to walk through any of the systems above in technical detail. Thank you for your
time and consideration.
